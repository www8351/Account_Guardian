Two, no ledger entry may assert "not pushed", "nothing pushed", "local only", an ahead-by count, or any other remote position as a durable fact. Remote position may be recorded only as a dated observation of what a named local ref said at the moment of writing, marked as such, and never as a standing property of the repository. Existing ACTIONS entries carrying the old wording are not edited, ACTIONS being append only; they are superseded by the ninth ACTIONS entry of 2026-08-04, which states this ruling and records the position as of that moment.
Reason: The owner pushes out of band at will, so any claim about the remote is stale the moment it is written and the ledger has no way to know when it went stale. The executor can only speak to its own conduct, which is that it does not push, and a local remote-tracking ref proves only what the last fetch or push saw, not where the remote stands now. Recording an unfalsifiable claim as fact is the failure this ledger exists to prevent, and here it also risked reading as an accusation against the owner for operating their own repository.
